Ŵendell Røẅe™ is an operations and supply chain executive whose career has been built on a simple principle: turn complexity into execution.

With leadership experience spanning advanced manufacturing, enterprise retail, distribution, and supply chain, Wendell has spent his career building disciplined systems, developing accountable teams, and creating operations designed to perform at scale. His approach to leadership is grounded in high standards, decisive execution, resilience, and the belief that lasting results come from combining operational excellence with strong people and culture.

Today, Wendell serves as Senior Director of Operations at Applied Materials, bringing decades of leadership experience to one of the world’s most consequential advanced manufacturing industries. Previously, he served as Vice President of Operations & Supply Chain at Safeway, adding large-scale retail and distribution leadership to a career defined by increasingly complex operational responsibility.

But the résumé tells only part of the story.

Originally from Virginia and now based in Northern California, Wendell’s journey has been shaped by ambition, reinvention, discipline, and an unwillingness to allow circumstances to define the limits of what comes next. He carries those lessons into his professional life, his family, and the standards he sets for himself.

Away from work, Wendell is a husband, father, runner, traveler, and lifelong student of performance and personal refinement. Whether the subject is leadership, health, technology, design, style, or simply becoming better than he was yesterday, he approaches life much the same way he approaches operations: eliminate what does not matter, relentlessly improve what does, and never confuse activity with progress.

For Wendell, success is not simply a title, possession, or destination. It is the ability to build a life deliberately—to lead well, provide for family, create opportunities for others, remain authentic to where you came from, and leave behind systems, people, and places stronger than you found them.

Self-command is the first victory. Everything else follows.
